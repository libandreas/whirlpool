How Whirlpool handles your information

Andreas Express develops Whirlpool PDF Search. This policy explains how Whirlpool handles your data.

Overview

Whirlpool reads and searches documents on your device, including text in scanned pages and images. Your files, their contents, and your searches are not sent to our servers or to an online AI service for processing.

The app may download files needed to read text and search documents on your device. These downloads do not send your document contents.

We do not sell your information, share your documents or searches with advertisers, or use them to train AI.

Information the app uses

  • The files and folders you select, including their names and file details
  • Text and images in your documents, including text recognized from scans
  • Your search terms, results, and the index used to find matches

All of this document and search data stays on your device. Settings, license checks, and usage statistics are described below.

When you connect a folder in the app window, Whirlpool can read that folder and its subfolders. It scans files on your device and indexes only supported documents, images, and text files.

What Whirlpool stores on your device

Whirlpool keeps working copies of your documents and the search index on your device so you can search them again next time you open the app.

Whirlpool uses an embedded WebView to display the app and stores app data locally on your device, including your settings and license information.

Checking your license and app notices

If you use a Gumroad license, Whirlpool sends your license key and the product being licensed to Gumroad to check that the license is valid. The app may repeat this check. Gumroad may return purchase details, such as your purchase email, which the app may display with your license status.

The app may also contact our website for version information and product notices. These checks and license checks do not include your documents or searches. Gumroad handles license information under its own privacy policy.

External connections are used to download files needed by the app, check licenses and notices, and collect the usage statistics described below. The services involved may receive basic connection information, such as your IP address.

Usage statistics

Whirlpool uses GoatCounter to count how often the app is opened. Each request includes the address of Whirlpool’s own page and a random value. It does not include your documents, search terms, or license key.

Protecting your information

Whirlpool follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements for handling user information. Connections to external services use secure connections.

This means we use your information only to provide and improve the app's stated features. We share it only when needed for those features, for security, or when required by law. We do not use it for personalized advertising.

We do not receive the documents you process on your device. If you send information to us for support, people may access it with your permission, when necessary for security, or when required by law.

Whirlpool uses the file permissions you grant when you select documents or connect a folder.

Keeping and removing your information

Saved copies and the search index stay on your device when you close the app window. They are removed when you delete them or clear the app’s stored data.

  • Use the app's option to delete saved document data to remove its working copies and search information.
  • Remove your license key in Settings.
  • Clear Whirlpool’s stored data on each device where you use it.

Clearing Whirlpool’s stored data leaves your original files in place.

External services keep information according to their own policies. To remove information they hold, use their account tools or contact them. You can contact us about information you sent directly to us.

Children

Whirlpool is not intended for children. Anyone who needs a parent or guardian's permission under the laws of their country should use it only with that permission.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the way it handles information changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer by email or use the contact form.

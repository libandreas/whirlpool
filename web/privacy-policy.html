Privacy policy

How Whirlpool handles your information

This policy explains how Whirlpool PDF Search, provided by Andreas Express, handles your information in the browser extension and web app.

Your documents and searches

Whirlpool reads and searches documents on your device, including text in scanned pages and images. Your files, their contents, and your searches are not sent to our servers or to an online AI service for processing.

The app may download files needed to read text and search documents on your device. These downloads do not send your document contents.

We do not sell your information, share your documents or searches with advertisers, or use them to train AI.

Information the app uses

Whirlpool works with files and folders you choose, their names and details, the text and images they contain, and your searches and results. It also uses your settings, license key, and purchase details returned when checking your license.

Choosing a folder lets the app read its files for searching on your device. Your browser may label the file selection button “Upload”, but choosing files does not upload them.

Browser storage

Whirlpool saves working copies of your documents and information used to search them in browser storage so you can use them again. These document copies and search information stay on your device and are not included in browser syncing.

Settings and license information are also saved in browser storage. Your browser may sync settings across your devices, depending on your browser settings. We do not run a service for storing or syncing your documents.

Checking your license and app notices

If you use a Gumroad license, Whirlpool sends your license key and the product being licensed to Gumroad to check that the license is valid. The app may repeat this check. Gumroad may return purchase details, such as your purchase email, which the app may display with your license status.

The app may also contact our website for version information and product notices. These checks and license checks do not include your documents or searches. Gumroad handles license information under its own privacy policy.

External connections are used to download files needed by the app, check licenses and notices, and collect the usage statistics described below. The services involved may receive basic connection information, such as your IP address.

Usage statistics

We collect usage information to understand how the app's features are used and identify problems. Document processing happens on your device, but the app also sends the usage information described below.

Whirlpool uses GoatCounter to count app openings, using the app page address and a randomly generated value. GoatCounter handles this information under its own privacy policy. Our website also uses it to measure visits.

This includes the app page address and messages shown by the app, such as notices, warnings, and errors. These messages may include details about what you are working on. The app may also send a valid license key to our website.

Our server also records connection details, including your IP address, country, browser language, browser and operating system details, and the time of the request.

We use country.is to identify the country associated with your IP address. This service receives your IP address for that purpose.

Our public website separately records visits, page and referring addresses, connection details, and messages shown by the website.

The Chrome Web Store may also provide statistics, such as how many people install the app, under Google's policies.

Protecting your information

Whirlpool follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements for handling user information. Connections to external services use secure connections.

This means we use your information only to provide and improve the app's stated features. We share it only when needed for those features, for security, or when required by law. We do not use it for personalized advertising.

We do not receive the documents you process on your device. If you send information to us for support, people may access it with your permission, when necessary for security, or when required by law.

The app relies on your browser's permissions and storage. Please protect your device and browser profile, especially when working with private documents.

Keeping and removing your information

Saved documents and search information remain in browser storage until you remove them or the browser clears them. Closing the app does not delete them.

  • Use the app's option to delete saved document data to remove its working copies and search information.
  • Remove your license key in Settings.
  • Clear the app's browser storage or uninstall the extension to remove its data from the device. If you also use the web app, clear its website data separately.

These actions do not delete the original files on your computer. Any synced settings are managed through your browser.

External services keep information according to their own policies. To remove information they hold, use their account tools or contact them. You can contact us about information you sent directly to us.

Children

Whirlpool is not intended for children. Anyone who needs a parent or guardian's permission under the laws of their country should use it only with that permission.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the way it handles information changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer by email or use the contact form.
